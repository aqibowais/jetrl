    Environment

    • Point-mass flight-path model at 30 Hz. A centered stick holds the flight path; pulling adds load factor up to what the speed allows, so a slow jet stalls and sinks. The nose sits along the velocity plus the angle of attack.
    • Observation (22): next two gates in the body frame, velocity, gravity direction, rates, throttle, altitude, speed, angle of attack, gate index, distance, g.
    • Action (4): throttle rate, pitch (load factor), roll rate, rudder.
    • Reward: progress toward the next gate, a pass bonus scaled by how centered it was, −40 for a crash, −20 for 20 s without a gate, and a lap bonus scaled by pace.

    In the browser

    jet_env.py is ported to TypeScript and the actor network runs as a plain matrix multiply on the exported weights, 30 times a second. The track generator is ported too, so New track flies circuits no policy has seen. A parity test replays a Python-recorded episode and requires the state to match every step to 10−9 m and the actions to match PyTorch to 10−4. Reported numbers come from Python.
